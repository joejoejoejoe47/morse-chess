# Lego Universe: credits

Every figure is an existing free LEGO model made by someone else. Nothing here was generated or built by us.
What we changed: each model's own geometry was regrouped into body/head/arm/leg parts around joint pivots so it can be animated in code.
Seated legs were stood up. The size was normalised to height 1, metal/gloss materials were made matte plastic, and the files were compressed with meshopt and webp textures (gltf-transform).

## Setting

- city.glb: the tower (building-skyscraper-d) and the city below are from Kenney's "City Kit (Commercial)" 2.1. They are CC0, https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial.
  The real Avengers Tower models found (Sketchfab CC BY: "Avengers Tower 001" by Leo.Leung, "THE AVENGERS TOWER" by MauricioFlores, "Avengers Tower" by DT_BRICKS) need a Sketchfab login to download, so this Kenney tower stands in for now.
- The balcony, glass rail, studded baseplate, glowing "A" sign, dusk sky and all effects (beams, portals, shockwaves, brick bursts) are simple shapes drawn in code.
